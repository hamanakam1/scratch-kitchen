# Cottage Compliance — Flag, Not Legal Advice

Cottage food rules, food-handler requirements, labelling, and permitted foods vary by state and county, and by sale vs. giveaway.

Before ANY cottage sale:
1. Confirm the current California / Marin County cottage food requirements from an official source.
2. Confirm the permitted foods list and labelling rules from that same official source.
3. Record that check, with date and source, in the app's compliance log.

Giveaway and sale are separate tracks, never relabelled.
This document is not legal advice, and no code in this repo certifies a food as safe.
