# Share Board

States: planned → packed → offered → picked up → delivered, plus cancelled.
Tracks: giveaway (free, people in need) and cottage sale (separate labelling/pricing/compliance), never relabelled.
Giveaway candidates must be: good or use soon only, not expected to be eaten at home before going off, safely prepared/packed, and approved by the cook for that specific batch/item/quantity/date.
No automatic dispatch, no payment, no courier booking in v1. A named human coordinator completes pickup/delivery.
